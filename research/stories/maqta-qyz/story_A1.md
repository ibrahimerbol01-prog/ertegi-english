TITLE: Maqta Qyz and the Cat
LEVEL: A1
TARGETS: cat, tail, milk, egg
---
[1] Maqta Qyz cleans the house. She finds a raisin. She calls the cat. The cat does not come. She eats the raisin.
[2] The angry cat drops her sour milk. Maqta Qyz cuts its tail.
[3] "Give me my tail!" says the cat. "Bring me milk," says Maqta Qyz. The cow wants leaves. The tree wants water. The girls want gum. The shop man wants an egg. The hen wants grain.
[4] The cat is tired. Does it stop or go on?
[5] A mouse gives it grain. The hen gives an egg. The egg gets gum, water, leaves and milk.
[6] The cat brings the milk. Maqta Qyz gives the tail back.
