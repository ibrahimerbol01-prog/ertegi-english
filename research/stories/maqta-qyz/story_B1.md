TITLE: Maqta Qyz and the Cat
LEVEL: B1
TARGETS: cat, tail, milk, egg, tree, grain, mouse, leaves
---
[1] One morning Maqta Qyz was cleaning her house when she found a sweet raisin. She wanted to share it, so she called the cat. But the cat did not come. When it did not answer, the girl ate the raisin herself and told the cat to keep away.
[2] A little later the cat came in and asked why she had called. Maqta Qyz refused to say. The cat said that it would spill her qatyq, a kind of sour milk. The girl said that she would cut off its tail. Neither of them stopped: the cat spilled the milk, and Maqta Qyz cut off the cat's tail.
[3] "Give my tail back!" the cat begged. Maqta Qyz said that she would return it only when the cat brought her new sour milk. So the cat went to the cow, but the cow was hungry and wanted fresh leaves. The tree was thirsty and asked for water. Some girls had water, but they wanted chewing gum. The shopkeeper wanted an egg for the gum, and the hen wanted grain for the egg.
[4] The road was getting longer and longer, and the cat was more tired than ever. Should it give up, or should it keep trying?
[5] The cat kept trying. While it was walking, it met a mouse that was digging its home. The mouse had a plate of millet, and it kindly gave the cat a handful of grain.
[6] Now everything moved back along the road. The hen ate the grain and gave an egg. The shopkeeper took the egg and gave gum. The girls took the gum and gave water. The tree drank the water and gave leaves, and the cow ate the leaves and gave milk. When the cat brought the sour milk, Maqta Qyz gave back its tail.
[7] A small quarrel can make a long road; sharing and honest work make it short again.
