TITLE: Maqta Qyz and the Cat
LEVEL: A2
TARGETS: cat, tail, milk, egg, tree, grain
---
[1] One day Maqta Qyz cleaned her house. She found a sweet raisin. "Cat, come here!" she called. But the cat did not come, so she ate the raisin herself.
[2] Later the cat came and asked, "Why did you call me?" Maqta Qyz did not tell it. The cat was angry, so it spilled her sour milk. Then Maqta Qyz was angry too, and she cut off the cat's tail.
[3] "Please give me my tail!" cried the cat. "Bring me sour milk first," said the girl. The cat ran to the cow. "I am hungry. Bring me leaves," said the cow. The tree said, "I am thirsty. Bring me water." The girls wanted gum. The shop man wanted an egg. The hen wanted grain.
[4] The cat was tired and sad. Should it stop, or should it try again?
[5] The cat tried again. A small mouse gave it some grain. The hen got the grain and gave an egg. The shop man got the egg. The girls got the gum. The tree got water.
[6] The tree gave leaves, and the cow gave milk. The cat brought the milk to Maqta Qyz, and she gave back its tail. After that they were friends.
