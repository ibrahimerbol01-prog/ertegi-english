TITLE: Hit, Club!
LEVEL: A2
TARGETS: goose, pot, donkey, club, children, magic
---
[1] A poor old man and his wife lived by setting traps. One day the old man found a goose in a trap.
[2] He felt sorry, so he let it go. "Come after a year," said the goose, "and ask for anything." His wife was angry, but the old man waited.
[3] After a year he went. He understood that the goose was rich Kazbai. Kazbai gave him a magic pot. When he said, "Cook, pot!", it made food. On the way some children asked him in for a meal. He went inside. Then the children used the pot and changed it for a plain one. He went back to Kazbai. This time he got a magic donkey that made gold. But the children changed the donkey too.
[4] The old man was sad. Should he give up, or should he go back one more time?
[5] He went back, and Kazbai gave him a magic club. It hit when someone said, "Hit, club!" The children tried it, and the club began to hit them.
[6] "Please stop it!" they cried. "We will give back the pot and the donkey." The old man stopped the club. He took the pot, the donkey and the club home.
[7] Kindness comes back to you. What is stolen does not stay with the thief.
