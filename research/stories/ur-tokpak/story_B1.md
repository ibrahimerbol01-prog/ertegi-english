TITLE: Hit, Club!
LEVEL: B1
TARGETS: goose, pot, donkey, club, children, magic, trap, words
---
[1] Long ago a poor old man and his wife lived on what they caught in their traps. One day, when the old man was checking a trap, he found a goose caught in it.
[2] He felt sorry for the bird and took it out of the trap. The goose told him that he could come after a year and ask for anything he wanted. His wife scolded him, but a year later the old man set off.
[3] On the road he met a herdsman with many camels. They belonged to a rich man called Kazbai. The old man guessed that Kazbai was the goose. A shepherd boy told him that Kazbai had three magic things. There was a pot that cooked any food, a donkey that made gold and a club that hit people. Each of them obeyed special words. Kazbai gave the old man the pot. On his way home some children who were playing knucklebones invited him in for a meal. He warned them never to say the pot's words. But while he was inside, they said the words and cooked food. Then they swapped the magic pot for a plain one. The same thing happened with the donkey.
[4] Twice the old man had come back with nothing. Was it better to give up, or to ask Kazbai one more time?
[5] He asked again, and this time Kazbai gave him the club. Again the children waited until he was inside and said the words: "Hit, club!" The club jumped up and began to hit them.
[6] The children begged the old man to stop it and promised to return everything. He called the club back, and they brought him the real pot and the real donkey. The old man went home with all three magic things.
[7] A kind act is repaid, and things taken by a trick are lost by a trick.
