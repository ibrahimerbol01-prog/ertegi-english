TITLE: Hit, Club!
LEVEL: A1
TARGETS: goose, pot, donkey, club
---
[1] An old man is poor. A goose is in his trap.
[2] He lets the goose go. "Come in a year," says the goose.
[3] A year later the goose gives him a magic pot. The pot cooks food. Some children take the pot. Then the goose gives him a magic donkey. The children take the donkey too.
[4] The old man is sad. Does he stop or go again?
[5] He goes again. The goose gives him a magic club. The children say, "Hit, club!" The club hits them.
[6] "Stop!" they cry. They give back the pot and the donkey. The old man takes all three home.
[7] Help others, and do not take what is not yours.
